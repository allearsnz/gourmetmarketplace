Returns Policy

Last updated January 2025

We want you to be happy with everything you order from Gourmet Marketplace. If something is not right, here is how returns work.

30-Day Window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

What Can Be Returned

  • Unopened, shelf-stable goods in original packaging.
  • Damaged or defective items: please contact us within 7 days of delivery so we can replace or refund.

What Cannot Be Returned

  • Perishable goods that have been opened.
  • Gift cards.
  • Clearance and final-sale items.
  • Custom and bulk-order items unless damaged in transit.

How to Initiate a Return

Email info@gourmetmarketplace.co with your order number and a brief description of the issue. We will respond within one business day with return instructions and a prepaid label if applicable.

Refund Timing

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receipt of the returned item. Funds typically appear on your original payment method within an additional 3–5 business days, depending on your bank.
